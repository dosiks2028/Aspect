Behind the scenes

About Row Five

A tiny movie club website that helps you stop scrolling and start watching.

Why Row Five?

Every evening the same thing happens: you open a streaming app, scroll for forty minutes and watch nothing. We wanted a calmer way to choose.

In a cinema, row five is the sweet spot: not too close, not too far. Our website is built on the same idea. You tell us your mood, and we give you one good movie.

  • 5 pages in one cinema hall
  • 2 questions to get your ticket
  • 0 hours wasted on scrolling
